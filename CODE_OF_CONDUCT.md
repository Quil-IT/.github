# Code of Conduct

Quil-IT adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) for all of its repositories and community spaces.

## In short

- Be respectful and constructive, in code review and everywhere else.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or unwelcome behaviour of any kind.
- Respect privacy: never share someone's personal or client information without permission.
- Assume good intent, and give feedback on the work, not the person.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **quilit.team@gmail.com**. All reports are reviewed promptly and handled in confidence. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code, and may block repeat offenders.
